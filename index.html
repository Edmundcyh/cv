About

As supply chain manager at eco-shop Malaysia, I look for ways to get clearer visibility across the supply chain. I also work on simplifying store replenishment, growing same-store sales, giving colleagues better tools, and working closely with stakeholders across the business.

Before eco-shop, I spent over a decade at Watsons, Tesco and Keppel Logistics. In those roles I covered stock purchasing, ordering and promotion forecasting, distribution-centre contracts, and trucking operations.

I hold a master's in supply chain management from the Malaysia Institute for Supply Chain Innovation (MISI), which is affiliated with MIT. I also have a certificate in AI and machine learning. I want to combine good supply chain practice with AI so the business, its customers and the environment all benefit.
