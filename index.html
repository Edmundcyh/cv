Approach

How I work

Every recommendation is grounded in the business's own data and designed so the people running the operation can take it over.

  1. Diagnose

    Start from the data to find where availability, inventory or cost is actually lost.

  2. Simplify

    Remove steps and exceptions before adding tools. Simple processes are easier to scale.

  3. Deliver

    Put changes in place with commercial, distribution and store teams, and measure the results.

  4. Build capability

    Leave behind the tools, reports and skills the team needs to keep improving.

About

Edmund Chong

I am a supply chain practitioner. My work sits where retail operations, data and AI meet.

I am Supply Chain Manager at eco-shop Malaysia, where I work on supply chain visibility, store replenishment and same-store sales growth. Before that, I led supply chain planning at Watsons and spent six years at Tesco. There I ran ordering and promotion forecasting, and managed distribution-centre income and supplier contracts. I began my career in distribution and trucking at Keppel Logistics.

I hold a Master of Science in Supply Chain Management from the Malaysia Institute for Supply Chain Innovation (MISI), part of the MIT Global SCALE Network. I also earned a certificate in AI and machine learning with The University of Texas at Austin.

“My goal is to combine the best practices of supply chain and artificial intelligence to deliver value to the customers, the business and the environment.”
